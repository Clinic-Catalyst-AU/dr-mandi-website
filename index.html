The clinic

Calm, clinical
and quietly confident.

Dr Mandi Cosmetic Skin Clinic has been part of Caroline Springs for six years - a doctor-led practice built around one belief: the plan should be shaped by your skin, never by whichever machine happens to be free that day.

Every pathway starts with a genuine, unhurried skin assessment. From there, a tailored plan draws on a medical-grade device suite - laser, ultrasound, microneedling, LED and regenerative treatments - chosen for your skin and your pace.

About Dr Mandi

Medicine first.
Aesthetics, by devotion.

MBBS · FRACGP · DCH · DRANZCOG · Dip. Aesthetic Medicine (AAAM)

Dr Mandi Sandhu is a practising General Practitioner and Cosmetic Physician. Her days are spent in real medicine - and it's that clinical grounding she brings to every aesthetic consultation. Skin is examined the way a doctor examines it: thoroughly, honestly, and with your whole health in view.

She has a special interest in pigmentation, skin rejuvenation and regenerative medicine, and has spent six years building a clinic where patients are assessed, not sold to.

"A proper skin assessment, before anything else. The plan is built around your skin - never around a machine."
