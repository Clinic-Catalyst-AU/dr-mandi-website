Our approach

Considered,
from the first look.

Cosmetic treatment at this clinic is medicine, practised carefully. Dr Mandi is a practising General Practitioner and Cosmetic Physician - your facial anatomy, medical history and suitability are assessed the way a doctor assesses them, before anything is planned.

The consultation is where the real work happens. Your face is studied at rest and in movement, your goals are heard properly, and an honest, tailored plan is built - including, sometimes, the advice that less is more, or that a different pathway would serve your skin better.

"The best result is the one nobody can name. You look rested, fresh - like yourself on a very good day."

Where your plan calls for prescription skincare, Dr Mandi works with a compounding pharmacy - high-quality, TGA-approved actives, prescribed in clinic and compounded for your skin alone by the clinic’s partner pharmacy. Australian advertising regulations mean prescription brand names and prices are never published. Your options, suitability and investment are discussed openly and precisely at your consultation - as they should be.

The private gallery

Her work is seen
after your consultation.

You won't find before-and-after photos of prescription cosmetic treatments on this website - and that's deliberate. Australian advertising guidelines (AHPRA) restrict how doctors may publicly display images of prescription-only treatments, because your decision should be shaped by clinical advice, never by advertising.

So Dr Mandi keeps her treatment gallery private. After your consultation, you're given personal access - a considered look at her actual work, viewed with context, honest guidance and your own features in mind.

"The rules protect you. And they make the gallery what it should be - a clinical reference for patients, shared in the right setting."

Common questions

Asked, and answered honestly.

What do prescription cosmetic treatments cost? +
Advertising rules mean we can't list brand names or prices publicly, and they're always tailored to you. Dr Mandi discusses your options, suitability and pricing properly at your consultation - openly and precisely, with nothing hidden.
Why can't I see before-and-after photos first? +
Australian advertising guidelines restrict doctors from publicly displaying images of prescription-only treatments - the rules exist so your decision is guided by clinical advice rather than marketing. After your consultation, you receive access to Dr Mandi's private gallery, where her work can be viewed properly and in context.
How long do results last? +
That varies from person to person, and it's always tailored - so Dr Mandi goes through what to expect for your face and your plan at consultation, rather than giving a one-size answer.
Will I look overdone? +
That result is never the goal here. The clinic's whole philosophy is fresh and natural - a rested version of you. Treatment is planned conservatively and built gradually, and Dr Mandi will tell you honestly when less is more.
Can I have cosmetic treatments while pregnant or breastfeeding? +
This is exactly the kind of question we review carefully as a doctor-led clinic, because suitability depends on your individual circumstances. Book a consultation and Dr Mandi will advise you properly.
